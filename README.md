<h2 id="overview">📊 Overview</h2>

<p>
The <b>India Supply Chain Performance Dashboard</b> is a business intelligence project designed to analyze supply chain performance using transaction-level data.
</p>

<p>
The project uses <b>Microsoft Excel</b> and <b>Power BI</b> to transform raw supply chain data into dashboards, KPIs, charts, and management-oriented analysis.
</p>

<p>
The analysis focuses on sales, demand forecasting, transportation costs, plant performance, distributor performance, lead time, and delivery performance.
</p>

<hr>

<h2 id="objectives">🎯 Project Objectives</h2>

<ul>
<li>Analyze overall sales performance</li>
<li>Compare forecast and actual sales</li>
<li>Measure sales achievement against forecast</li>
<li>Analyze transportation costs across plants and distributor cities</li>
<li>Compare planned lead time with actual lead time</li>
<li>Monitor monthly sales trends</li>
<li>Analyze product and plant performance</li>
<li>Review distributor-city performance</li>
<li>Monitor delivery and delayed shipments</li>
<li>Create an interactive management dashboard</li>
</ul>
